`NotificationClient::get_notification` and `NotificationClient::get_notifications` now compute the
push actions of a notification once, on the final event and after the decryption attempt, so that
the push rules are always evaluated on the cleartext when the event could be decrypted; previously
they could be evaluated on the still-encrypted event, where only the `.m.rule.encrypted*` rules can
match and no mention can be detected. When the notification client can't build a push context on
its own, or can't decrypt an event, it now falls back to the parent client, instead of reporting
unknown push actions (`is_noisy`, `has_mention` and `actions` left unset).
