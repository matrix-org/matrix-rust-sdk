Add `SqliteStoreConfig::high_entropy_passphrase` and the `Secret::HighEntropyPassPhrase` variant it builds, for randomly generated passphrases. The store caches a copy of its cipher in the new `cipher_fast` key/value entry under a cheap HKDF expansion and opens with that instead of running PBKDF2 every time. Do NOT use it with human-chosen passphrases as they would lose their brute-force protection.

The PBKDF2-wrapped `cipher` entry is still written and never replaced so this is interchangeable with `SqliteStoreConfig::passphrase`.
