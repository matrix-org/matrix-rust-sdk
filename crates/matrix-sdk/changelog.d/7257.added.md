Added support for [image packs](https://spec.matrix.org/v1.19/client-server-api/#image-packs).
`Room::image_packs()`, `Room::image_pack()` and `Room::set_image_pack()` read and write the
`m.room.image_pack` state events of a room. `Account::image_pack_rooms()`,
`Account::observe_image_pack_rooms()`, `Account::enable_image_pack_globally()` and
`Account::disable_image_pack_globally()` manage the `m.image_pack.rooms` account data.
`Room::available_image_packs()` returns all the packs available in a room, in the order
recommended by the spec: globally enabled packs, then the room's own packs, then the packs of
its canonical space hierarchy.
`Room::available_image_packs()` only reads from storage. To fetch the globally enabled packs and
the packs of the canonical space hierarchy from the server, call `Room::load_image_packs()` first.
