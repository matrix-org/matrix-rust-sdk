Room keys received from the `OlmMachine` are now collected in a dedicated task
while the event cache's redecryptor (R2D2) is busy, and processed once it
becomes available.

Previously, a busy redecryptor - for example, one working through a large batch
of events - could lag behind the room key broadcast channel and miss keys,
leaving events in the timeline undecrypted even though their keys had arrived.
