and `VersionBuilderError::NativeVersionIsUnset` are renamed to `Version::Unstable`,
`VersionBuilder::Unstable`, `VersionBuilder::DiscoverUnstable` and
`VersionBuilderError::UnstableVersionIsUnset`, as they refer to the unstable version of
