Accept the inviter's [MSC4268](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4268)
room key bundle after a server-initiated join (an auto-accepted invite or an
accepted knock), as proposed in
[MSC4590](https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4590). If the
invite was not part of the sync response, for example with sliding sync or after
a limited sync, the inviter is looked up in the user's own membership events
through `/messages`, and an already-received bundle is imported right away.
