Added support for image packs: `Room::image_packs()`, `Room::available_image_packs()`,
`Room::load_image_packs()`, `Room::set_image_pack()`, `Client::image_pack_rooms()`,
`Client::subscribe_to_image_pack_rooms()`, `Client::enable_image_pack_globally()`,
`Client::disable_image_pack_globally()` and `is_valid_image_pack_shortcode()`.
`AccountDataEventType` and `AccountDataEvent` have a new `ImagePackRooms` variant.
