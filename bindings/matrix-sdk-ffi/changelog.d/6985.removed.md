[**breaking**] Removed the `TimelineEventFilter` wrapper, as well as `FilterTimelineEventType` and `FilterTimelineEventCondition`. 
Equivalent types are now exposed in the FFI layer from the `matrix_sdk_ui` crate:

- `TimelineEventFilter` with `Include` and `Exclude` variants instead of `include` and `exclude` methods.
- `TimelineEventType` instead of `FilterTimelineEventType`. This previously took either a `MessageLike` or a `State` 
  variant with `MessageLikeEventType` and `StateEventType` respectively, now you just need to use `TimelineEventType`.
- `TimelineEventCondition` instead of `FilterTimelineEventCondition`.
