Add `SqliteStoreBuilder::high_entropy_passphrase`, a faster alternative to `SqliteStoreBuilder::passphrase` for randomly generated passphrases. The stores cache a copy of their cipher under a cheap derivation and open with that instead of running the expensive one every time. Do NOT use it with human-chosen passphrases as they would lose their brute-force protection.

It is interchangeable with `SqliteStoreBuilder::passphrase` so a client with a randomly generated passphrase migrates by calling this instead.
